The Anticipatory Threshold

Humanitarian Predictive Analytics Simulation
SESSION · STANDBY
Operations Briefing

You are the Operations Director. You need to make a decision affecting four countries under uncertainty.

As the Operations Director of a humanitarian agency, you need to make decisions about anticipatory action across four concurrent crises: drought and dispersed shelter needs in Country A, an epidemic with a critical data blind spot in Country B, armed conflict and forced displacement in Country C, and stalled post-earthquake recovery in Country D. Each country provides a different quantity and quality of data. You will move through thirteen steps across four phases: diagnosis and governance, political friction, operational constraints, and accountability. Your choices will shape the consequences of disasters and crises in these four countries.
